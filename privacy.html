Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version: Uvani doesn't sell your data, doesn't show ads, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. Uvani's AI voice features run on your iPhone. If you share a calendar with your household, the shared calendar is stored securely in the cloud so everyone's phones stay in sync.

This policy explains what information the Uvani app ("Uvani," "we," "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Uvani is operated by JAXIE LLC.

Information stored only on your iPhone

  • Your voice recordings. They are never uploaded, and only play on the phone where they were recorded.
  • Your events and settings, when you are not sharing a household calendar.
  • AI voice speech, which is created on your iPhone using Apple's built-in voices.
  • Your Tasks list, which is never shared or synced.

Information stored in the cloud

When you create or join a shared household calendar, the following is stored using Google Firebase so it can sync between household members' phones:

  • Shared events, including titles, dates, times, notes, and color labels.
  • Photos you attach to shared events.
  • The names and colors you assign to people in your household.
  • A random household code and an anonymous identifier for your device. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • A notification token, so the app can send notifications to your phone.

Only members of your household can see your household's shared calendar.

Waitlist email

If you join the Uvani waitlist at uvani.app/join, we collect your email address so we can send you a confirmation and one email when Uvani launches. Your email is stored with MailerLite, the email service we use. We don't sell it or use it for anything else. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.

Permissions Uvani asks for

  • Microphone: to record voice reminders and to let you add events by speaking.
  • Speech recognition: to turn what you say into an event. This uses Apple's speech recognition, which Apple may process on its servers under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Notifications and alarms: to remind you of events.
  • Photos: only when you choose to attach a photo to an event.

You can turn any permission off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't use advertising or ad tracking.
  • We don't use your calendar data to train AI.

Service providers

Uvani uses Google Firebase to store and sync shared household data, and Expo to deliver notifications. These providers process data only to run these services for Uvani.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data stored only on your iPhone is deleted when you delete the app. Shared household data stays in the cloud so other household members keep their calendar. To have your shared household data deleted, email us at hello@uvani.app with your household code, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Children

Uvani is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
